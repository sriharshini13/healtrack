Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about appointments, billing, the patient portal, visiting, and more.

Appointment FAQs

The easiest way is online! Use our Online Booking Form anytime. Select your preferred doctor, date, time, and visit type (In-Person or Telemedicine).

You can also call our Central Scheduling line at [Scheduling Phone Number] during business hours ([Hours]).

Please bring the following:

  • Photo ID (e.g., Driver's License)
  • Your current Insurance Card(s)
  • A list of all current medications (including dosage), vitamins, and supplements
  • Any relevant medical records, test results, or imaging reports if referred from another provider
  • A form of payment for potential co-pays or deductibles (See Insurance & Billing )

For new patients, please arrive 15-20 minutes early to complete registration paperwork. You might also be able to complete some forms via the Patient Portal beforehand.

We understand plans change. Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.

You can call our Scheduling line at [Scheduling Phone Number]. Some appointments may also be managed (canceled or rescheduled) through the Patient Portal if you have an account.

Failure to provide sufficient notice may result in a cancellation fee.

Telemedicine allows you to have a virtual appointment with your doctor using secure video conferencing from your computer, tablet, or smartphone.

It's suitable for many follow-ups, medication checks, consultations for certain minor illnesses, and mental health visits. You will receive instructions on how to connect prior to your scheduled virtual visit. Learn more on our Services page .

Billing & Insurance FAQs

We accept a wide range of insurance plans. Please see our Insurance & Payments page for a list of major accepted providers.

Important: It's always best to confirm your specific plan's coverage and network status directly with your insurance company before receiving non-emergency care, as lists can change and plans vary.

We offer several ways to pay:

  • Online: Via the secure Patient Portal .
  • Phone: Call our Billing Office at 1-800-BILLING.
  • Mail: Send a check to the address on your statement.
  • In Person: At the Cashier's office ([Location]).

More details are on the Insurance & Payments page .

We offer financial assistance programs for eligible patients. Please contact our Financial Counselors at 1-800-FIN-AID to discuss your situation and learn about potential options, payment plans, or charity care.

Visit the Financial Assistance section on our Insurance page for more information.

Patient Portal & Records FAQs

You typically need an activation code to register. You can request one during your next appointment check-in/out, or by calling our Registration Desk at [Registration Phone Number].

Once you have the code, visit the Patient Portal Login page and look for a "Sign Up" or "Register" link.

The portal allows you to securely:

  • View parts of your medical record (diagnoses, allergies, medications)
  • See lab and test results (usually after provider review)
  • View upcoming appointments
  • Request prescription refills*
  • Send secure messages to your care team (non-urgent)*
  • Pay your bill online*
  • Update your contact and insurance information

*Availability of certain features may vary.

The Patient Portal provides access to summaries and recent results. For a complete copy, submit a formal request to our Health Information Management (Medical Records) Department.

Find the required authorization form [Link to Form page/PDF] or contact Medical Records at [Medical Records Phone Number] for instructions.
